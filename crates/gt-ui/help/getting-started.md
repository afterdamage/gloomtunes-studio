# Getting started

GloomTunes Studio makes music from **patterns**: short loops of notes for the channels in the
channel rack. The **playlist** arranges patterns and audio clips into a song, and the **mixer**
shapes the sound.

## A first beat

1. Press **F6** for the channel rack. The starter project has a kick, a snare, a hat and a clap.
2. Click the step buttons to switch steps on and off.
3. Press **Space** to play. Press it again to pause.
4. Change the tempo with the **BPM** field, or click **Tap** on the beat a few times.

## Saving

**Ctrl+S** saves the project. **File > Save as template…** keeps it as a starting point:
**File > New** then offers it next to an empty project.

## Where things are

- **F5** playlist, **F6** channel rack, **F7** piano roll, **F9** mixer, **F8** browser.
- Any of these can float in its own window (the **Float** button next to the view tabs) and be moved
  to another monitor. The layout is restored next time.
- **Settings** has the audio device, MIDI, shortcuts, theme, UI size and language.
